The first playable release of **Ashen Vigil**.

**Download `AshenVigil.zip` below**, unzip it, and run `AshenVigil.exe` (Windows, 64-bit). If Windows warns about an unknown publisher, click **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's in it
- **Round-based survival:** the dead rise outside and tear through boarded breaches. Rebuild the barricades and hold the line.
- **Six enemy types:** Hollows, Ghouls, Bloated Husks, Wraiths, Revenant Knights, and Shade Hound rounds, plus elites.
- **Melee weapons:** wall weapons, the Cursed Reliquary, and the Soul Forge for elemental upgrades.
- **Seven perk altars and six power-ups.**
- **Source-style movement:** air strafing and bunny hopping.
- **Co-op for up to 4:** online by room code, or over LAN. Downed players can be revived.
